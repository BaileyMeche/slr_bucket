Figure 1. Time-series evolution of arbitrage spreads, 2019–2021. Each panel shows the cross-series mean (solid line) and ±1 standard deviation band (shaded) of absolute spread |W| in basis points, smoothed with a 5-day rolling average. The gray shaded region denotes the SLR exclusion relief period (April 1, 2020 – March 31, 2021). Dashed vertical lines mark the entry (red) and exit (green) dates.
