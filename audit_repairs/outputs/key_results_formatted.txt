MAIN FINDING
========================================================================

  The exit result is the paper's cleanest finding:
    Post x TreasuryBased = +3.85*** bps (SE=0.90, t=4.3)
    at W=60 (stable at W=20 and across TOTAL vs DIRECT specs).

  Interpretation: After SLR exclusion expired (2021-03-31),
  Treasury-based arbitrage spreads widened by ~3.9 bps more
  than non-Treasury spreads, consistent with balance-sheet
  constraints re-emerging as the exclusion was withdrawn.

  The entry compression (-2.2 bps) is directionally correct
  but insignificant and contaminated by COVID crash pre-period.
  The clean DiD (2019 baseline) resolves this ambiguity.
